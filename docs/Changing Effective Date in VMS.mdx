# Changing Effective Date in VMS (Moving Date Forward in Time)

Procedure for Processors to change a member's policy effective date to a later date in the Vantage Membership Service (VMS).

## Prerequisites

* Effective dates must be the first of the month.

## Context

:::caution Important
Customer Advocates (CAs) must refer to the RMO Workaround Pending Member Inquiries for On Off Marketplace procedure instead of using the steps below.
:::

Use these steps to change a member's effective date to a later date in VMS.

## Steps

1. Open the Customer Service Inquiry panel.  
   In the VMS menu bar, click **Processing > Customer Service**. Select 'Effective Date change' as the Inquiry Reason and enter the member's ID.
2. Change the effective date for the Primary Insured and all Dependents.  
   * Modify the Eligibility Coverage (medical and dental) effective date to the new date. This includes the Effective Date, Product Effective Date, and Original Effective Date (if applicable).
   * Modify the Ancillary Coverage Info effective date to the new date.
3. Update the effective dates for related records.  
   * Modify the policy Broker Assignment effective date.
   * Modify the active Funding Assignments effective date.
   * Modify the Contacts effective date.
   * Modify the Subsidy Maintenance effective date (if a record exists).
4. Request new ID cards for non-HMO members.  
   HMO member ID cards will be automatically generated after the PCP is updated.
5. Finish the Customer Service Inquiry and send a confirmation letter.  
   Click 'Finish Inquiry', select 'Closed on First Contact', and follow the RMO Workaround Creating Free Form Correspondence in CUI procedure.
6. If an HMO policy, update the effective date on the member's providers using the RMO Changing a Provider procedure.

## Result

The member's policy effective date is successfully moved forward in VMS across all relevant coverage and assignment records.
